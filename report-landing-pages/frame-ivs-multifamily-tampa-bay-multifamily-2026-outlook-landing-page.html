Market Snapshot

The State of
the Market.

Tampa is working through its overbuild phase. Deliveries peaked in 2024 and pulled occupancy to its lowest level in 24 years, while rents turned negative. With few new starts, absorption is next. Long term, housing supply will not keep up with Florida's population growth.

The Bottom Line

Five Things to Know
for 2026.

01Occupancy dipped to 89.6%.
Tampa multifamily occupancy fell to 89.6% in 2025, its lowest level since 2001, setting up recovery as supply slows.
02Rents are slipping.
Tampa rent growth was −2.8% in 2025, trailing Florida and the rest of the Southeast.
03The pipeline is emptying.
Very few new starts. Absorption continues into 2027 as deliveries fall.
04Values have reset.
Tampa multifamily sales volume fell 73% from 2021 to $1.7B in 2025.
05Regulators will push resolution.
After nearly four years of extensions, expect more foreclosures and more transactions in 2026.
